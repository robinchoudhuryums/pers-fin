REGRESSION RISKS: (1) Every spending figure drops by the month's refunds — intended, but historical months change too (any refund in the window), so a user comparing today's dashboard with an old screenshot / Sheets archive sees lower numbers. (2) A credit that is not a transfer, card payment or keyword-matched income — side income without a payroll keyword, a P2P app NOT_TRANSFER doesn't name, a cashback/statement credit — now reduces spending in its inherited category or "Uncategorized"; the per-category floor bounds the effect. (3) Budget snapshots older than the current refresh window keep their gross "spent" and rollover; only the prior month is re-taken on days 1–5. (4) The dashboard / Sheets month txn_count now counts distinct charges (a split parent once) — it used to count Sheets "lines".
