FOLLOW-ON ITEMS:
- Choices made where the plan left room (to confirm at A2): (1) "last payment smaller than the balance it was paying" needs the statement balance (stored from A8), so a last payment within 10% of the minimum stands in; (2) a LOAN at APR ≥ 8% joins the high-interest step (the plan names cards), and a loan with no APR isn't assumed 22% — it gets minimums only and a note; (3) the cash-floor cap applies only to money that leaves cash (debt, investing), not to the buffer / emergency-fund steps, whose money stays in cash; (4) the carried cards' and loans' minimums are taken off the surplus before the 60% (decision 2's "minimums always covered first") — conservative, since part of a carried card's minimum covers purchases already counted as spending; (5) the medium step's 50/50 halves aren't rounded to $25 (they can be $12.50 per paycheck).
- The emergency fund is measured against all spendable cash (checking + savings), not a named account; a goal marked "emergency fund" isn't read (A5's tracker).
- The avalanche assumes no new card purchases and a fixed minimum (real minimums fall with the balance), so payoff months are estimates.
- Card purchases on a carried card count in spending but its payments don't, so a household that carries a balance can read a larger "net" than the cash it keeps — the minimums subtraction offsets part of it.
- projections.computeLoanPayoff's default `today` is still the UTC date (a listed timezone-lint gap); advice.js doesn't call it.
